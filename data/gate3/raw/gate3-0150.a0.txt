Concern comes from needs across languages and settings, not from speaking Spanish, learning English, or combining languages. Bilingualism does not explain all the observed difficulties.

His play and interaction today offered useful information, but one speech-language visit cannot answer every developmental question. **Recomendaciones / Our next steps**

I recommend speech-language therapy through [[AGENCY|Little Learners Program]] with a bilingual clinician. Begin with two sessions each week, each lasting thirty minutes. Include caregiver coaching and coordination with daycare. Review participation and progress before changing frequency. The proposed service uses CPT [[KEEP|92507]] with modifier [[KEEP|GN]].

Treatment should focus on useful messages during play and routines. Begin with requesting help, describing a problem, and giving another person a short explanation. Support understanding through pictures, actions, and clear language. Increase difficulty gradually after success, rather than giving several new demands at once.

Use responsive interaction informed by [[KEEP|Hanen]] principles. Follow his interest, pause, respond to the message, and add a small amount of language. For example, when he says [[KEEP|"carro"]], model [[KEEP|"el carro corre"]]. Accept an attempt without requiring repeated imitation before he can continue playing.

At home, choose one brief routine each day. During snack, offer a choice and wait. During dressing, describe the problem and model a request. During books, comment on pictures instead of asking a question on every page. Keep practice playful: poquito a poquito, within activities the family already enjoys.

At daycare, Ms. [[TEACHER|Lopez]] can offer visual choices and a predictable turn-taking phrase. Miss [[TEACHER|Ana]] can pause before helping. La maestra [[TEACHER|Carmen]] can model Spanish during familiar songs and play. Adults should acknowledge the message first, then provide a fuller version without public correction.

Picture supports may help when frustration rises. This recommendation does not prescribe [[KEEP|PECS]] or [[KEEP|PROMPT]] at this time. The clinician should select a specific approach only after examining the skill it targets. Likewise, [[KEEP|Cycles]] may be considered later only if a fuller speech assessment supports that need.
