Based on standardized testing in both of [[CHILD|Majo]]'s languages, caregiver report, and classroom input from [[TEACHER|Ms. Londoño]], [[CHILD|Majo]] presents with a mixed receptive-expressive language disorder affecting both Spanish and English, consistent with [[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.2]].
