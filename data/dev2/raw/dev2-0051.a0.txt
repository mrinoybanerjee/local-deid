CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS / IMPRESIONES CLÍNICAS
- [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a moderate speech sound disorder affecting both English and Spanish, consistent with a true bilingual phonological disorder rather than a language difference, given that error patterns are present and comparable across both languages.
- He also presents with a mild-to-moderate expressive and receptive language delay in both English and Spanish, which cannot be attributed solely to typical second-language acquisition given the cross-linguistic consistency of deficits.
- [[KEEP|ICD-10]] codes considered: [[KEEP|F80.1]] (Expressive Language Disorder) and [[KEEP|F80.2]] (Mixed Receptive-Expressive Language Disorder), with [[KEEP|F80.0]] (Phonological Disorder) also applicable.
- [[KEEP|CPT codes]] for this evaluation: [[KEEP|92523]] (evaluation of speech sound production with evaluation of language comprehension and expression).
- Family history of late talking was noted; [[PARENT|Mrs. Fernández]] reports that her brother "tardó mucho en hablar" — took a long time to begin talking — and received speech therapy as a child, though no formal records are available.
- [[FAMILY|doña Lupe]] expressed concern that [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s difficulties might affect his confidence at school, stating "no quiero que se sienta menos que los otros niños" — she does not want him to feel less than the other children.
- Overall prognosis for improvement with consistent, intensive bilingual intervention is good, given [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s strong pragmatic skills, family engagement, and stimulability for target sounds with cueing.
