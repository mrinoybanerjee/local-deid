CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS

Based on standardized assessment results, caregiver and teacher report, and direct observation, [[CHILD|María José Hernández del Río]] presents with a mixed receptive-expressive language disorder and a moderate speech sound disorder affecting both her English and Spanish phonological systems. Her language difficulties are evident in both languages and therefore cannot be attributed solely to typical bilingual language development or to insufficient exposure to English; rather, the pattern of deficits meets criteria for a primary language impairment. This is further supported by family history of speech sound disorder and the consistency of error patterns across both languages during this evaluation.

Diagnostic impressions correspond to the following codes: Mixed receptive-expressive language disorder ([[KEEP|ICD-10: F80.2]]) and speech sound disorder ([[KEEP|ICD-10: F80.0]]). [[CHILD|Majo]]'s history of recurrent otitis media and tympanostomy tube placement, while now resolved audiologically, is noted as a likely contributing factor to her early speech sound development.
