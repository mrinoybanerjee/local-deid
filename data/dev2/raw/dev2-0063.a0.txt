SUMMARY

[[CHILD|Mateo]] is a [[KEEP|4;2]]-year-old bilingual Spanish/English-speaking boy referred by [[PROVIDER|Dr. Patel]] for a comprehensive speech and language evaluation due to parent and teacher concerns regarding delayed expressive language, reduced speech intelligibility, and difficulty following multi-step directions in both languages. [[CHILD|Mateo]] currently attends [[DAYCARE|The Caterpillar Clubhouse]] three days per week and receives Early Head Start home-visiting services. He is enrolled with the [[AGENCY|Mecklenburg County CDSA]] under an active IFSP, with [[PROVIDER|James Alvarez]] serving as service coordinator. Results of this evaluation indicate moderate delays in expressive language in both English and Spanish, mild-moderate delays in receptive language, and a moderate speech sound disorder affecting intelligibility, particularly in connected speech. Recommendations include continued speech-language therapy twice weekly, continuation of IFSP services, and a repeat audiological evaluation.

Phonological process analysis identified the following patterns:

- Cluster reduction (e.g., "[[KEEP|star]]" produced as [[KEEP|[tɑɹ]]])
- Fronting of velars (e.g., [[KEEP|/k/]] and [[KEEP|/g/]] produced as [[KEEP|[t]]] and [[KEEP|[d]]], respectively)

IMPRESSIONS

[[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a moderate mixed receptive-expressive language disorder, more pronounced in expressive language, affecting both English and Spanish, consistent with a true language delay rather than typical bilingual second-language acquisition patterns. He additionally presents with a moderate speech sound disorder characterized by multiple phonological processes affecting intelligibility in both languages, with reduced stimulability for several target sounds. Given his history of recurrent otitis media and NICU stay, hearing status should continue to be monitored closely despite the most recent normal audiogram from [[HOSPITAL|Piedmont Crossing ENT & Audiology – SouthPark Office]]. [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s social communication and play skills are age-appropriate and are a relative strength to build on in intervention.

Relevant diagnostic coding for this encounter includes [[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.2]] (Mixed receptive-expressive language disorder) and [[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.0]] (Phonological disorder), billed under [[KEEP|CPT 92523]] (Evaluation of speech sound production with evaluation of language comprehension and expression) and [[KEEP|CPT 92597]] (not applicable, removed) — correction: billed under [[KEEP|CPT 92523]] only, with modifier [[KEEP|GN]] applied per clinic policy for speech-language pathology services.
