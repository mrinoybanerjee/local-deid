IMPRESSIONS

[[CHILD|Mateo Hernández Cruz]] is a [[KEEP|4;2]]-year-old bilingual (Spanish-English) male presenting with a moderate-to-severe mixed expressive-receptive language disorder affecting both of his languages, alongside a moderate speech sound disorder characterized by fronting, gliding, and final consonant deletion patterns consistent with, but more persistent than, typical phonological development for his age. Importantly, [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s difficulties are present across both Spanish and English, which, per bilingual assessment best practice and corroborated by the [[KEEP|BESA]] bilingual composite scores, supports a true language disorder rather than a language difference attributable to typical second-language acquisition or sequential bilingual exposure.

[[CHILD|Mateo]]'s history of chronic otitis media with effusion during a developmentally sensitive period, combined with his [[KEEP|34-week]] prematurity and NICU stay, are considered likely contributing risk factors to his current profile, though his language and speech difficulties appear to extend beyond what would be expected from hearing history alone, particularly given his currently normal hearing sensitivity.

Reduced intelligibility, combined with family members such as [[SIBLING|Sol]] frequently interpreting on his behalf, appears to be contributing to [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s growing frustration and withdrawal from peer interactions at [[DAYCARE|Little Learners Bilingual Academy]] and during community activities such as the library storytime program. Continued compensatory "translation" by family members, while well-intentioned, may reduce [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s opportunities to practice independent communication and should be addressed through family coaching.

[[CHILD|Mateo]] meets eligibility criteria for Early Intervention services under the [[AGENCY|North Carolina Infant-Toddler Program]] based on standardized scores at least [[KEEP|1.5 standard deviations]] below the mean in both receptive and expressive language domains across both of his languages, as well as moderate impairment in speech sound production affecting intelligibility and social participation.
