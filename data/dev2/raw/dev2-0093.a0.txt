[[CHILD|Teo]] substituted [[KEEP|/k/]] and [[KEEP|/g/]] with [[KEEP|[t]]] and [[KEEP|[d]]] respectively in word-initial position (e.g., "[[KEEP|tat]]" for "[[KEEP|cat]]," "[[KEEP|dato]]" for "[[KEEP|gato]]"), consistent with velar fronting. 8. CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS

Results of today's bilingual evaluation indicate that [[CHILD|Teo]] presents with a Speech Sound Disorder and a Language Disorder affecting both Spanish and English, with deficits present across both languages that cannot be attributed solely to typical second-language acquisition or reduced exposure. Scores on the [[KEEP|PLS-5]] and [[KEEP|CELF-5 Spanish]] fall more than [[KEEP|1.5]] standard deviations below the mean in both languages, and speech sound production deficits (velar fronting, final consonant deletion, cluster reduction, inconsistent tap production) are present across both Spanish and English phonological systems.

[[CHILD|Teo]]'s strong nonverbal communication, social engagement, and relatively intact receptive vocabulary are considered relative strengths. His frustration with communication breakdowns, while currently manageable, is a risk factor for future behavioral concerns if left unaddressed.

Given the combined pattern of findings, [[CHILD|Teo]] meets criteria for Early Intervention services through [[AGENCY|Piedmont Regional CDSA]] under the category of Established Condition / Developmental Delay in Communication, and is recommended for an updated IFSP with speech-language goals as Outcome [[KEEP|#2]] and Outcome [[KEEP|#3]].

9. RECOMMENDATIONS

9.1 It is recommended that [[CHILD|Teo]] receive individual bilingual speech-language therapy services two times weekly, 30 minutes per session, focusing on expressive language structure, speech sound production, and functional communication strategies in both Spanish and English.

9.2 A formal audiological re-screening is recommended given the history of recurrent otitis media and tube placement, to rule out any residual hearing loss contributing to current language and speech sound delays. This can be coordinated through [[PROVIDER|Dr. Samuel Price]]'s office or through [[AGENCY|Piedmont Regional CDSA]].

9.3 Therapy should incorporate principles of [[KEEP|PROMPT]] for motor-based speech sound intervention and the [[KEEP|Cycles approach]] for phonological pattern remediation, with cueing hierarchies progressing from [[KEEP|maximum cues]] to [[KEEP|moderate cues]] (mod cues) to [[KEEP|minimal cues]] as [[CHILD|Teo]] demonstrates mastery at each level.

9.4 Given [[CHILD|Teo]]'s strong nonverbal communication and occasional frustration, introduction of a visual support system modeled on [[KEEP|PECS]] principles is recommended as a bridge strategy during periods of reduced intelligibility, to be phased out as verbal intelligibility improves.

9.5 Caregiver coaching using [[KEEP|Hanen]]-based strategies ("OWL": Observe, Wait, Listen) is recommended for [[PARENT|Lucía Rodríguez Pérez]], [[PARENT|Jesús Hernández Morales]], and [[FAMILY|Rosa Pérez Núñez]], given their central roles in [[CHILD|Teo]]'s daily language environment. Materials will be provided in Spanish.

9.6 It is recommended that [[CHILD|Teo]] continue bilingual exposure at home and at [[DAYCARE|Mariposa Azul Early Learning Village]]; no reduction of either language is indicated, as continued dual-language exposure supports, rather than impedes, his overall communication development.

9.7 Coordination between [[EMPLOYER|Carolina Bilingual Therapy Partners, PLLC]], [[DAYCARE|Mariposa Azul Early Learning Village]], and [[AGENCY|Piedmont Regional CDSA]] is recommended to ensure consistency of strategies across settings. A classroom consultation with Ms. [[TEACHER|Lopez]] is recommended within the next [[KEEP|30 days]].

9.8 Re-evaluation is recommended in approximately [[KEEP|6 months]], or sooner if significant changes in communication skills are observed, with the next IFSP review currently scheduled for [[DATE|15 de enero de 2027]] (numerically, [[DATE|01/15/2027]]).
