PRESENT LEVELS OF DEVELOPMENT

[[CHILD|Mateo]] communicates primarily through gestures, a small set of single words in Spanish and English, and vocal protests when frustrated. He understands simple directions in both languages when paired with gesture (e.g., "dame el perro" / "give me the dog"). He imitates environmental sounds and some animal sounds. He engages in brief parallel play with [[SIBLING|Sol]] but does not yet initiate turn-taking games independently. Family and daycare report he is social, affectionate, and motivated to communicate, but his limited intelligibility (estimated [[KEEP|30%]] intelligible to unfamiliar listeners) causes frustration several times daily.
