OUTCOMES

Outcome [[KEEP|1]]: [[CHILD|Majo]] will use two-word combinations to request preferred items/activities in Spanish and/or English during daily routines, increasing from current baseline of less than 5% of opportunities to 80% of opportunities across three consecutive data collection sessions, as measured by provider and parent report.
Strategies: Model core vocabulary during meals and play using [[KEEP|PROMPT]] tactile-kinesthetic cues; parent coaching using [[KEEP|Hanen]] "It Takes Two to Talk" strategies, specifically OWL (Observe, Wait, Listen) and self-talk/parallel-talk during bath and bedtime routines; introduce a low-tech visual choice board as a backup augmentative strategy if verbal requesting does not progress by the next quarterly check-in.

Outcome [[KEEP|2]]: [[CHILD|Majo]] will reduce frustration-related behaviors (crying, grabbing, pushing) during transitions and peer play at home and at [[DAYCARE|Jack-in-the-Pulpit Preschool]] from current baseline of 6-8 episodes/day to 2 or fewer episodes/day, measured by teacher and parent tracking logs.
Strategies: Teach a simple manual sign ("more," "help," "done") paired with spoken approximation; collaborate with [[TEACHER|Ms. Whitfield]] on a visual schedule for the toddler room; coach [[FAMILY|Abuela Ofelia]] and [[PARENT|Esteban]] on consistent use of the same signs/words at home to support generalization.

Outcome [[KEEP|3]]: [[CHILD|Majo]] will produce at least 3 of 5 target consonants (/m/, /n/, /p/, /s/, /t/) in word-initial position with [[KEEP|minimal cues]] across structured and semi-structured tasks, as measured by SLP data collection.
Strategies: Cycles approach targeting stimulable sounds in word-initial position; bilingual phonological contrast drills alternating Spanish and English target words; home practice sheet with 10 target words per week, tracked by parents with a simple tally (target: [[KEEP|8/10]] accuracy by next review).

Outcome [[KEEP|4]]: The family will identify and understand at least two Part B transition options and complete the transition conference prior to [[CHILD|Majo]]'s third birthday.
Strategies: [[PROVIDER|Yesenia Campos Ruiz]] will schedule the transition conference for [[DATE|February 2027]] and provide bilingual written materials on local preschool special education options; [[PROVIDER|Carmen Alicia Rivas]] will share a written summary of [[CHILD|Majo]]'s communication profile for the transition team.
