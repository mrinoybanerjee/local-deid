OUTCOMES / RESULTADOS

Outcome 1 / Resultado 1: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will use single words and approximations to request preferred items and people across settings.

Strategies: Family and providers will use naturalistic language modeling, expanding [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s gestures into words ("¿Quieres agua? Agua."). The SLP will use a core vocabulary board at home and daycare. Target words for the developing [[KEEP|/s/]] sound at the one-word level include [[KEEP|'sol']] (sun), [[KEEP|'silla']] (chair), and [[KEEP|'sopa']] (soup), practiced with [[KEEP|moderate verbal cues]] during structured play. During the most recent session, [[CHILD|Mateo]] approximated [[KEEP|'sol']] as [[KEEP|[tol]]] with [[KEEP|maximal cues]], a step toward the target [[KEEP|/s/]] phoneme. Doña [[FAMILY|Lupe]] and [[FAMILY|Rosa]] will be coached to model the same words during daily routines, since [[FAMILY|Rosa]] often sings "Que sale el sol" with him in the mornings.

Outcome 2 / Resultado 2: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will engage in reciprocal play with a familiar adult or peer for [[KEEP|3 of 5]] observed opportunities.

Strategies: Use of [[KEEP|Hanen's It Takes Two to Talk]] strategies with parents, including following [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s lead and commenting rather than questioning. Miss [[TEACHER|Ana]] will support brief parallel-play interactions between [[CHILD|Mateo]] and [[PEER|Jaden]] during free play. Weekly home visits by [[PROVIDER|Sarah Whitfield]] will model turn-taking games.

Outcome 3 / Resultado 3: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will transition into the toddler room at [[DAYCARE|Little Steps Early Learning Center]] with reduced distress, tolerating separation for [[KEEP|30 minutes]] without sustained crying.

Strategies: Gradual exposure visits, a visual schedule in Spanish and English, and consistent goodbye routines coached by the service coordinator, [[PROVIDER|Patricia Gómez]], and Miss [[TEACHER|Ana]].

Outcome 4 / Resultado 4: The family will connect with additional community support, including continued participation in the "Leamos Juntos" program at the [[ORG|Cumberland County Public Library]] and exploration of respite care options through [[AGENCY|Smart Start of Cumberland County]].
