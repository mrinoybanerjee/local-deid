**Resultados y Estrategias / Outcomes and Strategies**

**Outcome 1:** [[CHILD|Mateo]] will use two- to three-word combinations to request, comment, and protest across settings (home, daycare) in both Spanish and English, as measured by caregiver log and direct observation, by [[DATE|April 6, 2027]].

Strategies: [[PROVIDER|Marisol Vega]] will model expansion techniques drawn from [[KEEP|Hanen]]'s "It Takes Two to Talk" during biweekly home visits, coaching [[PARENT|Lucía]] and [[FAMILY|doña Lupe]] to expand [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s single-word utterances in Spanish during daily routines (bath time, meals). [[TEACHER|Miss Ana]] will use parallel talk and modeling in English during daycare activities. Home program materials will be provided in Spanish.

**Outcome 2:** [[CHILD|Mateo]] will produce final consonants in single words with no more than [[KEEP|minimal cues]], increasing intelligibility to familiar and unfamiliar listeners, as measured quarterly by probe data, by [[DATE|April 6, 2027]].

Strategies: [[PROVIDER|Marisol Vega]] will implement a modified [[KEEP|Cycles approach]] targeting final consonant deletion, incorporating [[KEEP|PROMPT]]-informed tactile cueing given [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s stimulability for [[KEEP|/p/]] and [[KEEP|/t/]]. Target words will be selected in both languages (e.g., "pelota"/"ball," "gato"/"cat") to support cross-linguistic transfer.

**Outcome 3:** The family will report at least two new strategies used successfully at home each month to support [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s communication and reduce frustration-related behaviors, as measured by caregiver interview, by [[DATE|April 6, 2027]].

Strategies: [[PROVIDER|Marisol Vega]] and [[PROVIDER|Denise Alvarez]] will coordinate a joint home visit with [[AGENCY|El Centro Hispano]] staff to connect [[PARENT|Lucía]] with a Spanish-language caregiver support group.
