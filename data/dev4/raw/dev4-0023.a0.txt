IMPRESSIONS / IMPRESIONES

[[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a severe mixed receptive-expressive language delay affecting both Spanish and English, consistent with a primary language impairment rather than a language difference attributable to dual-language exposure, given that delays are present and comparable across both languages. His speech sound system is also significantly delayed relative to chronological age expectations. [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s history of recurrent otitis media may have contributed to, but does not fully account for, the magnitude of his current delays, as his most recent hearing screening ([[DATE|07/18/2023]]) was within normal limits.

[[CHILD|Mateo]] qualifies for early intervention services under the North Carolina Infant-Toddler Program eligibility criteria based on standardized test scores more than [[KEEP|1.5 standard deviations]] below the mean in two domains (receptive and expressive language).
