**Impressions**

Results of this evaluation indicate a moderate mixed receptive-expressive language disorder affecting both English and Spanish, consistent with a primary language impairment rather than a language difference attributable solely to bilingual exposure, given that delays are present across both languages relative to same-age bilingual peers. [[CHILD|Sofía]] also presents with a moderate speech sound disorder characterized by multiple phonological process errors that significantly reduce intelligibility, particularly to unfamiliar listeners such as classroom staff at [[DAYCARE|Little Sprouts Early Learning Center]]. Her history of recurrent otitis media is a likely contributing factor to early language delay, though current hearing status is within normal limits and should not be considered an active barrier to progress.

[[CHILD|Sofía]]'s relative strengths in social communication, joint attention, and nonverbal communication are encouraging prognostic indicators. Her family's strong engagement, bilingual home environment, and existing connections with [[AGENCY|Piedmont CDSA]], [[AGENCY|Early Head Start]], and [[AGENCY|Guilford County Smart Start]] provide a solid foundation for continued intervention.
