CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS

Based on standardized assessment, parent and teacher report, and clinical observation, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a mixed receptive-expressive language disorder in the context of bilingual language exposure, with an associated phonological disorder. These findings are consistent with [[KEEP|ICD-10]] codes [[KEEP|F80.2]] (mixed receptive-expressive language disorder) and [[KEEP|F80.0]] (phonological disorder). His difficulties are present and comparable across both Spanish and English, which, combined with his bilingual home environment and typical exposure history, supports a true language disorder rather than a difference attributable to second-language acquisition or dialectal variation. [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s strong social engagement, play skills, and responsiveness to visual and gestural supports are favorable prognostic indicators.

RECOMMENDATIONS

1. [[CHILD|Mateo]] qualifies for ongoing speech-language intervention and should continue to receive services under his updated IFSP, with treatment addressing [[KEEP|IFSP Outcome 2]] (functional communication across settings).
2. Individual speech-language therapy sessions, billed under [[KEEP|CPT 92507]], are recommended at the frequency outlined below.
3. Treatment should incorporate [[KEEP|PROMPT]] for motor-speech planning given [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s inconsistent sound substitutions, and the [[KEEP|Cycles]] approach to target phonological patterns in a developmentally sequenced manner.
4. Given [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s apraxia-like inconsistency on repeated productions, a trial of the [[KEEP|Kaufman]] Speech to Language Protocol is recommended to build approximations toward functional words.
5. [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s family would benefit from parent coaching through a [[KEEP|Hanen]] program, specifically [[KEEP|It Takes Two to Talk]], to support language facilitation strategies across both Spanish and English during daily routines.
6. [[KEEP|PECS]] should be introduced as a supplemental communication support during high-frustration moments, with the explicit goal of fading as spoken language increases.
7. Continued collaboration with [[DAYCARE|The Curious Caterpillar Early Learning Center]] staff, including [[TEACHER|Ms. Lopez]], is recommended to generalize strategies to the classroom setting.
8. A re-evaluation is recommended in six months to monitor progress and adjust the treatment plan.
