ARTICULATION AND INTELLIGIBILITY

On the [[KEEP|GFTA-3]], [[CHILD|Mateo]] demonstrated multiple sound errors affecting intelligibility in connected speech. Phonological processes observed included final consonant deletion (e.g., "ca" for "cat" [[KEEP|/kæt/]]), cluster reduction (e.g., "poon" for "spoon"), and gliding of liquids (e.g., [[KEEP|[w]]] for [[KEEP|/r/]], as in "wabbit" for "rabbit"). In Spanish, [[CHILD|Mateo]] substituted [[KEEP|[d]]] for [[KEEP|/ɾ/]] in medial position, producing "pedo" for "pero," and simplified consonant clusters, producing "poblema" for "problema."

During a single-word naming probe in English, [[CHILD|Mateo]] correctly produced [[KEEP|8/10]] target words with final consonants present, approximately [[KEEP|80%]] accuracy, when given [[KEEP|moderate cues]] (verbal model plus visual cue). Without cueing support, accuracy dropped to [[KEEP|3/10]] ([[KEEP|30%]]). In spontaneous connected speech sampled during play with blocks and the family dog toy, [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s intelligibility to an unfamiliar listener (the examiner) was estimated at [[KEEP|40-50%]]; intelligibility to [[PARENT|Lucía Rodríguez-Flores]], a familiar listener, was estimated at [[KEEP|75-85%]].

[[CHILD|Mateo]] was observed to say "[[KEEP|[[KEEP|Rocky]] run]]" during a play sample, referring to the family dog, [[PET|Rocky]], chasing a ball; the target production was judged a close approximation with gliding present on the final consonant.

LANGUAGE AND VOCABULARY

Receptive and expressive language skills were judged to be below age expectations in both English and Spanish, with relatively stronger performance in Spanish consistent with greater input exposure in that language. [[CHILD|Mateo]] demonstrated understanding of basic spatial concepts (in, on, under) and two-step directions with [[KEEP|moderate cues]] but had difficulty with "wh-" questions, particularly "why" and "how" forms, answering correctly on only [[KEEP|2 of 5]] trials.

Expressively, [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s mean length of utterance was estimated at [[KEEP|1.4 morphemes]], consistent with an age equivalent of approximately [[KEEP|24-27 months]], well below his chronological age of [[KEEP|4;2]]. Utterances were coded in a language sample using [[KEEP|SALT]] conventions; several utterances were marked [[KEEP|[EW]]] for unintelligible word-level productions that could not be transcribed with confidence.

IMPRESSIONS

[[CHILD|Mateo Alejandro Flores]], a [[KEEP|4;2]]-year-old bilingual Spanish-English speaking male, presents with a mixed receptive-expressive language disorder ([[KEEP|F80.2]]) affecting both languages, with relatively greater deficits in expressive language and speech sound production. Standardized testing in both English and Spanish fell in the below-average to significantly below-average range across all measures administered, with scores and confidence intervals summarized above (see [[DATE|June 11, 2026]] and [[DATE|June 18, 2026]] administration dates). Articulation errors, phonological process patterns, and reduced intelligibility are consistent with a moderate speech sound disorder occurring alongside the language disorder. These findings are consistent with [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s current IFSP diagnosis and support continued, intensified intervention.

[[CHILD|Mateo]]'s stronger performance in Spanish relative to English is consistent with typical patterns of bilingual language development given his home language environment, and should not be interpreted as evidence of a language-based learning disability specific to English; rather, his deficits are present across both languages relative to same-age bilingual peers, supporting a true language disorder rather than a language difference.

RECOMMENDATIONS

1. [[CHILD|Mateo]] is a strong candidate for individual speech-language therapy services, recommended at a frequency of twice weekly, 30-minute sessions, billed under CPT codes [[KEEP|92507]] (individual treatment) and [[KEEP|92523]] (evaluation of speech sound production with evaluation of language comprehension and expression), consistent with ICD-10 diagnosis [[KEEP|F80.2]].
