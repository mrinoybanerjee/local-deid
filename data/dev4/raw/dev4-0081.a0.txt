Impresiones Clínicas / Clinical Impressions

Results indicate a mixed receptive-expressive language disorder, with expressive language more significantly affected than receptive language, in the context of typical bilingual development. [[CHILD|Teo]]'s difficulties are not explained by his bilingual exposure alone — children raised with two languages typically show comparable or only mildly reduced scores relative to monolingual peers once total vocabulary across both languages is considered, and [[CHILD|Teo]]'s expressive scores remain low even accounting for this. His articulation errors are developmentally appropriate for his age and are not an independent concern at this time, though they may contribute to intelligibility difficulties when paired with his limited expressive language.

[[CHILD|Teo]] presents as a socially engaged, affectionate child who uses nonverbal strategies (pointing, pulling, gesture) effectively to communicate needs, which is a meaningful strength to build on. His strong receptive language skills, in both English and Spanish, are also a significant asset. Family involvement is excellent: [[PARENT|Lucía]] and [[PARENT|Carlos]] are attentive, consistent, and eager to support [[CHILD|Teo]]'s communication, and extended family members, including [[FAMILY|Yolanda]] and [[CHILD|Teo]]'s cousins, provide additional rich language exposure.
