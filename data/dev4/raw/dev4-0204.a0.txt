interpretation and impressions

based on combined standardized and informal measures mateo presents with a speech sound disorder affecting intelligibility in both spanish and english with primary difficulty on /r/ /ɾ/ and consonant clusters he additionally presents with a language delay affecting expressive vocabulary and sentence structure in both languages though the degree of delay is likely less severe than english-only scores suggest given his bilingual status and language exposure history this profile is consistent with a true communication disorder present across both of mateo's languages rather than a difference attributable solely to second language acquisition given concerns were reported by caregivers in both the primary language environment and the daycare setting

mateo would be expected to benefit from direct intervention targeting speech sound production and expressive language in a manner that supports rather than suppresses his home language given the family's strong preference and need for spanish maintenance a bilingual approach to intervention is recommended family was previously familiar with [[KEEP|PECS]] from a cousin's experience and asked whether that might apply here it was explained that [[KEEP|PECS]] is typically used for children with minimal verbal output which does not describe mateo at this time clinician discussed several approaches that may be incorporated into mateo's plan including [[KEEP|Hanen]] style coaching to support mother's use of language facilitation strategies at home [[KEEP|PROMPT]] for tactile cueing of articulatory placement particularly for /r/ and clusters and the [[KEEP|Cycles]] approach for phonological pattern remediation given the pattern of cluster reduction observed the [[KEEP|Kaufman]] speech praxis approach was also discussed as a possible adjunct should motor planning difficulties emerge over the course of treatment and family was given information on [[KEEP|It Takes Two to Talk]] as a parent coaching resource available in spanish through [[AGENCY|Foothills CDSA]]
