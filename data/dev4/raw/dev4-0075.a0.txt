Outcomes and Strategies

Outcome 1: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will use two-word combinations to request and comment during daily routines at home and at [[DAYCARE|Buttercup Meadow Early Learning]].
Strategies: [[PROVIDER|Daniela Ruiz]] will coach [[PARENT|Lucia Hernandez]] and [[PARENT|Ruben Hernandez]] using [[KEEP|Hanen]] "It Takes Two to Talk" strategies, including following [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s lead, expanding his single words into two-word models, and building in pause time during snack and bath routines. [[TEACHER|Ms. Keisha Owens]] will use the same expansion strategy during daycare mealtimes and will track frequency weekly.

Outcome 2: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will produce initial [[KEEP|/s/]] and final consonants in single words with [[KEEP|mod cues]] in structured play, including his target word [[KEEP|"sol"]].
Strategies: [[PROVIDER|Daniela Ruiz]] will use a [[KEEP|PROMPT]]-informed tactile cueing approach during twice-weekly sessions, moving from [[KEEP|max cues]] toward [[KEEP|min cues]] as accuracy improves. The family will practice five minutes nightly using a short list of meaningful words, including [[KEEP|"sol"]], [[KEEP|"papa"]], and [[KEEP|"agua"]].

Outcome 3: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will use a gesture or word instead of hitting when frustrated with his sister or peers, in [[KEEP|4 of 5]] observed conflicts.
Strategies: [[PARENT|Ruben Hernandez]] and [[PARENT|Lucia Hernandez]] will model the sign for "more" and "help" at home, and [[TEACHER|Ms. Keisha Owens]] will prompt [[CHILD|Mateo]] with the same signs during conflicts with [[PEER|Amara]] and other classmates. [[SIBLING|Sol Hernandez]] will be coached by her parents to pause and offer [[CHILD|Mateo]] a choice of two toys rather than removing an item outright, which the family reports already reduces some conflicts at home.
