[[KEEP|Outcome 1]]: [[CHILD|Teo]] will use [[KEEP|2-word combinations]] to request desired items and activities during daily routines in at least [[KEEP|4 of 5]] opportunities, in English or Spanish, as observed by caregivers.

Progress will be reviewed at each visit using caregiver report and direct observation.
