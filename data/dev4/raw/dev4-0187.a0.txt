IMPRESSIONS

[[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a moderate expressive language delay and a moderate-to-severe phonological disorder affecting both Spanish and English, consistent with a true bilingual speech-language impairment rather than second-language acquisition alone, given that error patterns are present and comparable across both languages. His receptive language is a relative strength, though also below age expectations. Reduced intelligibility appears to be contributing to frustration, reliance on gesture, and dependence on his sister, [[SIBLING|Valentina Fuentes]], to communicate on his behalf.

[[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with:
