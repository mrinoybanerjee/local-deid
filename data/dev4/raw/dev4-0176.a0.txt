Impresiones clínicas. Based on today's assessment, parent and teacher report, and medical and developmental history, [[CHILD|Teo]] presents with a Mixed Receptive-Expressive Language Disorder, [[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.2]], co-occurring with a Phonological Disorder, [[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.0]], affecting both of his languages. CPT code [[KEEP|92523]] (evaluation of speech sound production with evaluation of language comprehension and expression) applies to today's assessment. His difficulties are evident across Spanish and English, which, per current best practice in bilingual assessment, supports a true language disorder rather than a language difference attributable to dual-language exposure, limited English proficiency, or typical bilingual developmental variation; this distinction is important given extended family's prior assumption that the delay was simply because he is "bilingüe." His prematurity, prolonged NICU course, and history of recurrent otitis media with documented conductive hearing loss episodes are all plausible contributing factors, though his most recent audiological evaluation and today's screening confirm hearing within normal limits bilaterally at present. [[CHILD|Teo]]'s strong nonverbal play skills, social engagement, and responsiveness to cueing during dynamic assessment are favorable prognostic indicators.
