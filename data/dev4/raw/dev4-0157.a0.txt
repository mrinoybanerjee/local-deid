IMPRESSIONS / IMPRESIONES CLÍNICAS

[[CHILD|Teo]] presents with a Mixed Receptive-Expressive Language Disorder affecting both Spanish and English, consistent with a true language impairment rather than second-language acquisition alone, given his significant delay relative to typical bilingual development milestones and the degree of delay documented even in his dominant language, Spanish. He additionally presents with a Speech Sound Disorder characterized by a restricted phonemic inventory and reduced intelligibility across both languages, with error patterns that are not fully attributable to typical phonological interference between Spanish and English.

Relevant diagnostic codes include [[KEEP|F80.9]] (Developmental disorder of speech and language, unspecified) and [[KEEP|R62.0]] (Delayed milestone in childhood). Billing for this evaluation was submitted under CPT codes [[KEEP|92523]] and [[KEEP|92522]].

Given his history of prematurity, NICU admission, and recurrent otitis media, medical and audiological factors likely contributed to his early communication delays. However, given that his otitis media has been resolved for over a year and his most recent hearing screening was within normal limits, his current language and speech sound profile reflects an ongoing, clinically significant communication disorder that warrants continued, intensive intervention rather than a wait-and-see approach.
