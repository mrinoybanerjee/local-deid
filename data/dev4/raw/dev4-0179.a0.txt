A: (ASSESSMENT / EVALUACIÓN)

Results indicate a moderate-to-severe expressive language disorder and a moderate phonological disorder affecting both Spanish and English, consistent with a true bilingual language/speech sound disorder rather than typical second-language acquisition or dialectal variation. Error patterns are present and proportionally similar across both languages, which, combined with below-average receptive scores and family history, supports a disorder rather than a difference. [[CHILD|Teo]] meets criteria consistent with ICD-10 codes [[KEEP|F80.1]] (expressive language disorder) and [[KEEP|F80.0]] (phonological disorder); mixed type given co-occurring deficits.

Diagnoses support continued Part C eligibility and an updated IFSP. Severity and bilingual profile warrant services delivered primarily in Spanish, the home language, with English language stimulation incorporated as [[CHILD|Teo]]'s exposure increases at [[DAYCARE|Little Sprouts Bilingual Daycare]]. Frustration behaviors reported by mother and [[TEACHER|Ms. Lopez]] are consistent with communication frustration secondary to the disorder, not a primary behavioral or social-emotional diagnosis at this time; recommend monitoring.

Strengths: age-appropriate play skills, intact joint attention and social reciprocity, stimulable sound inventory suggesting good prognosis with intervention, supportive and engaged family, and existing bilingual exposure that should be leveraged rather than reduced.
