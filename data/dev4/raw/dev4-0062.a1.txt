IMPRESSIONS

Results of this bilingual evaluation indicate that [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a Mixed Receptive-Expressive Language Disorder ([[KEEP|F80.2]]) affecting both English and Spanish, along with a moderate speech sound disorder characterized by multiple phonological processes. Because deficits are present across both of [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s languages, his language difficulties cannot be attributed to typical bilingual language acquisition or dialectal variation alone. His scores on the PLS-5 fell in the Low to Below Average range across receptive and expressive domains, and his BESA Morphosyntax score likewise fell Below Average, supporting a true language impairment rather than a language difference.

[[CHILD|Mateo]]'s speech sound errors, including final consonant deletion, stopping, and gliding, are consistent with a phonological disorder that is moderately impacting intelligibility, particularly for unfamiliar listeners such as classmates [[PEER|Ethan Moore]] and [[PEER|Sofia Price]] and extended family including [[FAMILY|Ana Flores]]. These intelligibility concerns are contributing to reported frustration and social withdrawal observed by [[TEACHER|Jennifer Lopez]] at school and by [[FAMILY|Rosa Delgado]] during afternoon care.

[[CHILD|Mateo]]'s attention, play skills, and nonverbal communication appear to be relative strengths, and his engagement with novel adults, including the clinician and interpreter, was appropriate for his age. His motor-based concerns, addressed by [[PROVIDER|Allison Brooks]] and [[PROVIDER|Kevin Walsh]], do not appear to be significantly limiting his participation in speech-language intervention at this time.
