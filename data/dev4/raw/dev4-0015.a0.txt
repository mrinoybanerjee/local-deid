IMPRESSIONS / IMPRESIONES CLÍNICAS

Based on standardized assessment, parent and teacher report, and dynamic/informal measures, [[CHILD|Teo]] presents with a Developmental Language Disorder affecting both Spanish and English, with low average to below average performance across comprehension, expression, and morphosyntax in both languages relative to bilingual Spanish-English normative data. His pattern of errors is consistent with a true language disorder rather than typical second-language acquisition or dialectal variation, given that deficits are present across both languages and persist beyond what would be expected from English exposure alone. He additionally presents with a moderate speech sound disorder affecting intelligibility in both languages, characterized by gliding, cluster reduction, and final consonant deletion, patterns that affect connected speech more than isolated word production.

[[CHILD|Teo]] would benefit from direct, bilingual speech-language intervention addressing both expressive/receptive language and speech sound production. Given the family's strong Spanish-language home environment and the importance of maintaining Spanish for family and cultural connection — including upcoming travel the family is planning to visit relatives in [[PLACE|El Guayabo]] next year — therapy should be delivered using a bilingual, cross-linguistic approach rather than English-only intervention.
