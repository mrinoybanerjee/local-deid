CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS

[[CHILD|Teo]] presents w/ a Mixed Receptive-Expressive Language Disorder affecting BOTH Spanish and English, consistent across multiple standardized measures (PLS-5, BESA) and confirmed via clinical observation and parent report across settings ([[DAYCARE|Little Sprouts Early Learning Academy]], home, extended family). Performance is depressed relative to bilingual peer norms in both the heritage language (Spanish, primary home lang) and the emerging second language (English), which per BESA bilingual guidelines and clinical judgment rules out typical bilingual language variability/second-language acquisition lag as sole explanation.

Co-occurring moderate-severe Speech Sound Disorder noted, characterized by multiple phonological process patterns (final consonant deletion, liquid simplification, cluster reduction, occasional velar fronting) resulting in significantly reduced intelligibility (approx [[KEEP|30%]] to unfamiliar listeners). Given pt's age ([[KEEP|3;2]]) and severity/pattern of errors, concern for underlying phonological disorder rather than solely developmental delay is warranted; several patterns (liquid simplification, cluster reduction persisting at this age) exceed what would be expected developmentally for a monolingual or bilingual [[KEEP|3;2]]-yo per normative data reviewed.

Pt's use of gesture as primary communicative mode, limited symbolic play, and reported frustration/behavioral escalation when not understood are consistent w/ functional impact of combined expressive language and speech sound deficits on daily communication and social participation (at home, daycare, and with peers [[PEER|Ethan Combs]] and [[PEER|Sofia Nguyen]]).

No red flags for autism spectrum disorder identified during this eval (e.g., pt demonstrated joint attention, functional play, social referencing to CG and clinician); however, given overall profile, continued monitoring across domains via the multidisciplinary CDSA team (OT eval w/ [[PROVIDER|Rachel Kim]], PT screening already completed by [[PROVIDER|James Patterson]]) is recommended, and any emerging social-communication concerns should be flagged promptly to SC [[PROVIDER|Priya Nair]] and pediatrician [[PROVIDER|Dr. Samuel Osei]].
