[[KEEP|Outcome 2]]: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will produce final consonants in single words with [[KEEP|no more than moderate cues]] in 8 of 10 opportunities.

[[KEEP|Outcome 3]]: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will use a backup visual system to reduce frustration-related biting at daycare within three months.

[[KEEP|Outcome 4]]: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will increase vowel accuracy and syllable shape variety to support later multisyllabic word attempts.
Strategies: [[KEEP|Kaufman]] Speech to Language Protocol hierarchy for motor-planning practice, beginning with reduplicated syllables before moving to true words; coordinate with [[PROVIDER|Brian Walsh, OTR/L]] regarding oral-motor tone findings noted during his evaluation.
