SUMMARY AND CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS

[[CHILD|María José Fuentes Rivas]] is a [[KEEP|4;2]]-year-old bilingual Spanish-English speaking girl who presents with a mixed receptive-expressive language disorder affecting both of her languages, alongside a speech sound disorder characterized by fronting, final consonant deletion, and liquid simplification patterns beyond what is expected for typical bilingual development. Her difficulties are not attributable solely to normal second-language acquisition, as deficits are present in Spanish, her dominant and home language, as well as in English. The combination of reduced intelligibility, limited utterance length, and gaps between comprehension and expression is consistent with findings reported by her family, doña [[FAMILY|Lupe]], and her teachers, la maestra [[TEACHER|Carmen]] and Miss [[TEACHER|Ana]], at [[SCHOOL|Eastside Elementary School]].

Diagnostic impressions: Mixed Receptive-Expressive Language Disorder ([[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.2]]) and Phonological Disorder ([[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.0]]). These findings support continued eligibility for speech-language intervention services under [[AGENCY|Piedmont Regional CDSA]] and warrant consideration for continued services through [[SCHOOL|Guilford County Schools]] at the time of her transition from Part C to Part B services.

Services provided during this evaluation are billed under [[KEEP|CPT 92523]] (evaluation of speech sound production with evaluation of language comprehension and expression) and [[KEEP|CPT 96125]] (standardized cognitive performance testing, interpretation and report).
