IMPRESIONES CLÍNICAS / CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS

Based on standardized testing, conversational sampling, and parent and teacher report, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a mixed receptive-expressive language disorder and a concomitant speech sound disorder affecting both Spanish and English, his two languages of exposure. His difficulties are evident across languages rather than limited to one, which rules out a primary difference related solely to second-language acquisition; per [[KEEP|BESA]] conceptual scoring guidelines, his performance falls below expectations even when the stronger response across languages is credited, consistent with a true bilingual language disorder rather than typical bilingual development. [[PARENT|Lucía]] and [[PARENT|Hugo]]'s report that [[CHILD|Teo]] "entiende todo" (understands everything) is only partially consistent with testing, which showed receptive scores also below age expectations, though stronger than his expressive scores. His speech sound errors, including final consonant deletion, stopping, gliding, and cluster reduction, are contributing significantly to his reduced intelligibility and are consistent with a phonological, rather than purely articulatory, pattern, affecting both of his languages. Diagnostic impressions are consistent with [[KEEP|Mixed Receptive-Expressive Language Disorder (ICD-10 F80.2)]] and [[KEEP|Phonological Disorder (ICD-10 F80.0)]].

This evaluation was billed under [[KEEP|CPT 92523]] (evaluation of speech sound production with evaluation of language comprehension and expression).
