Articulation and Phonology

[[CHILD|Mateo]] demonstrated a restricted phonetic inventory in both languages. In English, he produced final consonant deletion in approximately [[KEEP|60%]] of opportunities and stopping of fricatives, substituting [[KEEP|/t/]] for [[KEEP|/s/]] and [[KEEP|/d/]] for [[KEEP|/z/]]. Both error patterns were consistent with developmental phonological processes but were judged more frequent and more persistent than typical for his age in both languages.

CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS

Based on standardized testing, informal measures, parent report, and clinical observation, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a bilingual language disorder affecting both Spanish and English, along with a moderate speech sound disorder affecting intelligibility in both languages. His receptive and expressive language scores fall in the [[KEEP|moderately to severely delayed range]] in English, and his informal Spanish measures similarly indicate delay relative to same-age bilingual peers. His difficulties are not attributable to hearing loss, given normal audiological findings, nor solely to typical bilingual language-learning patterns, given that his delays are present and comparable in magnitude across both of his languages.

[[CHILD|Mateo]]'s phonological error patterns, including final consonant deletion, stopping, and difficulty with Spanish rhotics, are contributing significantly to reduced intelligibility, particularly for unfamiliar listeners. These patterns, combined with reduced mean length of utterance and limited grammatical morpheme use, are consistent with a diagnosis of:
- [[KEEP|Developmental Language Disorder]], expressive-receptive, affecting both languages (ICD-10 [[KEEP|F80.2]])
- [[KEEP|Phonological Disorder]] (ICD-10 [[KEEP|F80.0]])

CPT codes for this evaluation include [[KEEP|92523]] (evaluation of speech sound production with evaluation of language comprehension and expression) and [[KEEP|92521]] (evaluation of speech fluency), modifier [[KEEP|-GN]].
