IMPRESSIONS

[[CHILD|María José Mendoza Rivas]] presents with a moderate speech sound disorder and mild-to-moderate expressive and receptive language delay in both English and Spanish, consistent with a diagnosis of Speech Sound Disorder ([[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.0]]) and Developmental Language Disorder ([[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.1]]). Her difficulties are present in both languages, which rules out the pattern typically seen in typically developing bilingual children whose difficulties are isolated to the non-dominant language.

Her history of prematurity, NICU stay, and recurrent otitis media are plausible contributing risk factors for both the speech sound disorder and the broader language delay. Performance across both English and Spanish measures, combined with parent and teacher report, supports a true bilingual communication disorder rather than a language difference secondary to limited English exposure. [[CHILD|María José]]'s stronger relative performance in Spanish is consistent with her home language environment and should guide treatment language allocation moving forward.
