Impressions

Based on standardized testing, language sampling, parent and teacher report, and direct observation, [[CHILD|Teo]] presents with a moderate expressive and receptive language delay affecting both Spanish and English, alongside a moderate phonological disorder affecting intelligibility in both languages. His scores fall more than [[KEEP|1.5 standard deviations]] below the mean in both languages, which, combined with error patterns that do not fully resolve with cueing and persist across both languages, support a diagnosis beyond what would be expected from typical bilingual language development alone. His ICD-10 diagnostic impression is [[KEEP|F80.9]] (Developmental disorder of speech and language, unspecified) with a secondary impression of [[KEEP|F80.0]] (Phonological disorder). Relevant CPT codes for today's evaluation include [[KEEP|92523]] (evaluation of speech sound production with evaluation of language comprehension and expression) and [[KEEP|96110]] (developmental screening, with scoring) for the CDI checklist, modifier [[KEEP|-59]] appended to reflect the distinct procedural service.

[[CHILD|Teo]]'s mild low tone, noted by [[PROVIDER|Kevin O'Brien]] and [[PROVIDER|Sarah Whitfield]], likely contributes to reduced oral-motor precision underlying some articulation errors, supporting continued interdisciplinary collaboration. His strong social engagement, joint attention, and responsiveness to both languages are significant strengths and predict a favorable response to continued intervention, particularly peer-mediated approaches given his emerging interest in [[PEER|Juanito]] and classroom participation.
