IMPRESSIONS / IMPRESIONES CLÍNICAS

[[CHILD|Pepe]] presents w/ a Mixed Receptive-Expressive Language Disorder (ICD-10: [[KEEP|F80.2]]), evident across both his languages, consistent w/ a primary language impairment rather than a language difference attributable solely to bilingual exposure. Speech sound disorder (ICD-10: [[KEEP|F80.0]]) also present, impacting intelligibility in both English and Spanish w/ similar error patterns across languages, supporting a true phonological disorder rather than typical bilingual phonological variation. Concomitant mild dysfluency features noted but do not currently meet criteria for a primary fluency disorder; recommend re-screen in [[KEEP|6 months]].

Given bilingual presentation in both languages at comparably depressed levels, this pattern supports a language disorder construct rather than a language-difference/second-language-acquisition explanation. Family's language model is rich and appropriate; no evidence that reduced input or recent language exposure accounts for the magnitude of delay observed.
