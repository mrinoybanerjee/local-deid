CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS / IMPRESIONES CLÍNICAS

[[CHILD|Teo]] presents with a moderate-to-severe mixed receptive-expressive language disorder affecting both Spanish and English, consistent with a true bilingual language impairment rather than typical second-language acquisition. This impression is supported by below-average performance across both home and school language measures, history of prematurity and recurrent otitis media, and converging parent and teacher report. Co-occurring speech sound disorder affecting both languages is also present, with difficulty at the phoneme level in [[KEEP|/k/, /g/, /ɾ/]], and [[KEEP|/l/]].
