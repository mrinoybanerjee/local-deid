IMPRESSIONS / IMPRESIONES CLÍNICAS

[[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a significant delay in both receptive and expressive language skills across English and Spanish, consistent with a diagnosis of [[KEEP|Developmental Language Disorder]], affecting both languages rather than reflecting typical bilingual second-language acquisition patterns. His delay is evident on standardized measures in his dominant language (Spanish) as well as in informal English sampling, ruling out the possibility that scores reflect limited English exposure alone. Scores across the [[KEEP|PLS-5]] Bilingual Edition, [[KEEP|PPVT-5]] Spanish form, and [[KEEP|Bayley-4]] Language subtests fell consistently in the [[KEEP|1st to 3rd percentile]] range, indicating delays of approximately [[KEEP|12-16 months]] below chronological age expectations.

[[CHILD|Mateo]]'s history of prematurity, NICU stay, and early conductive hearing loss are all known risk factors contributing to his current language delay, though his hearing has since normalized. His limited expressive vocabulary, reliance on gesture, and emerging but inconsistent speech sound production (particularly difficulty with velars and liquids in both languages) are consistent with his overall language profile and are not attributable to oral-motor dysfunction or current hearing loss.

Given [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s age, the severity of his delay, and his risk history, he meets eligibility criteria for Part C early intervention services through [[AGENCY|Mecklenburg County CDSA Office]] under the established condition/informed clinical opinion pathway as well as the standard deviation criteria for his age.
