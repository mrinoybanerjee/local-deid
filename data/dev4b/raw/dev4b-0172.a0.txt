Receptive and Expressive Vocabulary: On informal probes, [[CHILD|Teo]] demonstrated a vocabulary that, when combined across both languages (conceptual scoring), fell closer to age expectations than when either language was scored in isolation, a pattern consistent with typical bilingual development rather than a primary language disorder.

IMPRESIONES CLÍNICAS / CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS

[[CHILD|Teo]] presents with a speech sound disorder and expressive language delay that are evident across both of his languages, which supports a true, underlying disorder rather than a difference attributable solely to bilingualism or dialectal variation. His receptive language skills are a relative strength, falling within the low-average to below-average range, while expressive language and speech sound production fall below age expectations in both English and Spanish.

Diagnosis: Speech Sound Disorder ([[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.0]]) and Language Disorder, Expressive-Receptive ([[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.2]]).

[[CHILD|Teo]]'s pattern of code-switching, observed throughout testing and reported at home, is consistent with typical bilingual language use and should not be targeted for reduction; rather, it reflects appropriate cross-linguistic flexibility given his dual-language environment. His difficulties are best explained by a disorder affecting both of his languages concurrently.
