Impressions / Impresiones

Based on standardized testing, parent and teacher report, and clinical observation, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a moderate-to-severe mixed receptive-expressive language disorder (ICD-10 [[KEEP|F80.2]]) with a co-occurring speech sound disorder affecting both his English and Spanish phonological systems. His difficulties appear consistent across both languages, which supports a true disorder rather than a typical bilingual language difference. His receptive skills, while also delayed, remain relatively stronger than his expressive skills, suggesting he understands significantly more language than he can produce.

[[CHILD|Mateo]]'s reduced intelligibility, estimated at [[KEEP|30-40%]] with unfamiliar listeners, is likely contributing to his increasing frustration and reliance on gesture rather than verbal attempts, as described by both [[PARENT|Ana Fernández-Soto]] and his teacher, [[TEACHER|Ms. Ortiz]]. His strong play skills, joint attention, and social engagement with peers such as [[PEER|Noah]] are notable strengths that should support therapy progress.

Diagnostic impression supports continued eligibility for speech-language services through [[AGENCY|CDSPI]] under his existing IFSP, as well as initiation of direct speech-language therapy through this clinic to address both expressive language and speech sound production in both languages.

Recommendations / Recomendaciones

1. Individual speech-language therapy, twice weekly for [[KEEP|30 minutes]] per session, focusing on phonological awareness, word-final consonant production, and expansion of expressive vocabulary and sentence structure in both English and Spanish.
2. Continued home language stimulation strategies, coached for [[PARENT|Ana Fernández-Soto]] and [[PARENT|Carlos del Río]], using a [[KEEP|Hanen]]-based "It Takes Two to Talk" framework adapted bilingually.
3. Collaboration with [[DAYCARE|Little Sprouts Bilingual Daycare]] staff, including [[TEACHER|Ms. Ortiz]] and [[TEACHER|Mr. Fuentes]], to reinforce target sounds and encourage verbal requesting during classroom routines.
4. Coordination with [[AGENCY|CDSPI]] service coordinator [[PROVIDER|Marisol Vega]] regarding updated IFSP outcomes and frequency of services.
5. Referral to occupational therapy, [[PROVIDER|Jennifer Boyd]], OTR/L, for a brief consultation regarding oral-motor function and feeding, given mild reports of picky eating that may warrant monitoring.
6. Re-evaluation in [[KEEP|6 months]] to monitor progress and adjust the treatment plan, including repeat administration of the [[KEEP|PLS-5]] Spanish Edition.
7. Follow-up with pediatrician [[PROVIDER|Dr. Patel]] to rule out any additional contributing medical factors, including recurrent otitis media history.

[[CHILD|Mateo]]'s family was counseled extensively on the distinction between bilingual language difference and true language disorder, and both parents verbalized understanding and expressed strong motivation to participate in home carryover activities. [[PARENT|Ana Fernández-Soto]] specifically requested that materials be provided in Spanish when possible, which this clinician will coordinate with the family moving forward.
