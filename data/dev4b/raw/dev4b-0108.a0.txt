IMPRESIONES / IMPRESSIONS

Based on standardized testing, parent and teacher report, and clinical observation, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a Speech Sound Disorder (ICD-10 [[KEEP|F80.0]]) and a Mixed Receptive-Expressive Language Disorder (ICD-10 [[KEEP|F80.2]]) affecting both English and Spanish. Scores fell consistently in the below-average to well-below-average range across both receptive and expressive domains in both languages, indicating a true bilingual language impairment rather than a language difference secondary to dual-language exposure. His difficulties with intelligibility, sentence length, and vocabulary are present across both languages, with his history of recurrent otitis media considered a likely contributing factor to early language acquisition delays.

[[PARENT|Ana Sofía Fernández]] shared, "Nos preocupa que no lo entiendan en la escuela" ("we worry that he won't be understood at school"), and this concern is clinically supported by today's findings. [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s current profile places him at risk for continued academic and social difficulty without intervention, particularly given the upcoming transition out of early intervention.

[[CHILD|Mateo]] continues to meet criteria for ongoing early intervention services through [[AGENCY|Piedmont CDSA]] and is expected to transition to preschool exceptional children's services through the local school district prior to kindergarten entry. Coordination between [[PROVIDER|Denise Shull]], the family, and [[DAYCARE|Manzanita Kids Academy]] staff will be important during this transition period.
