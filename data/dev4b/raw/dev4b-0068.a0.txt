Age-equivalent range on the PLS-5 Expressive Communication subtest fell between [[KEEP|2;6–3;0]], and GFTA-3 performance corresponded to an age equivalent of [[KEEP|2;9–3;3]].

On conversational language sample analysis, [[CHILD|Mateo]] produced mean length of utterance of [[KEEP|2.8 morphemes]], below expectations for his chronological age. Articulation testing revealed a moderate phonological disorder affecting both languages. [[CHILD|Mateo]] demonstrated fronting of velars (target [[KEEP|/k/]] produced as [[KEEP|[t]]]), final consonant deletion, and gliding of liquids, substituting [[KEEP|[w]]] for [[KEEP|/ɾ/]] in Spanish words such as "pe[[KEEP|[w]]]o" for "pe[[KEEP|/ɾ/]]o" (perro). Receptive vocabulary and following of two-step directions in both languages fell within the low range but were relatively stronger than expressive skills, consistent with a mixed receptive-expressive presentation weighted toward expressive deficits.

IMPRESIONES CLÍNICAS / CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS

[[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a moderate-to-severe mixed receptive-expressive language disorder and a moderate phonological disorder affecting both English and Spanish, consistent with a true bilingual language impairment rather than typical second-language acquisition patterns; errors were present and comparable across both languages rather than confined to the non-dominant language, which rules out typical bilingual variation as the sole explanation. His history of prematurity, NICU stay, and recurrent otitis media are relevant contributing factors but do not fully account for the magnitude of delay observed.
