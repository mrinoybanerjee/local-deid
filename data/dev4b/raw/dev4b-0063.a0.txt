Articulation and Phonology

Single-word articulation testing in English revealed inconsistent production of word-final consonants and stopping of fricatives. [[CHILD|Mateo]] produced [[KEEP|/k/]] correctly in word-initial position with [[KEEP|min cues]] but substituted [[KEEP|[t]]] for [[KEEP|/k/]] word-finally, saying "[[KEEP|cat]]" as "[[KEEP|cat]]" with the target "[[KEEP|cat]]" reduced to "[[KEEP|ca]]." In Spanish, he demonstrated difficulty with the tap [[KEEP|/ɾ/]], producing "[[KEEP|pero]]" as "[[KEEP|peo]]," consistent with typical developmental patterns but delayed relative to age expectations. During a home-language sample collected with his mother, [[CHILD|Mateo]] was coded for mean length of utterance using SALT codes, including two instances of [[KEEP|[EW]]] marking word-finding difficulty.

Impressions

Based on standardized testing, language sampling, and caregiver and teacher report, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a moderate expressive and receptive language delay affecting both Spanish and English, consistent with a diagnosis of expressive-receptive language disorder (ICD-10 [[KEEP|F80.2]]). His articulation skills are also delayed relative to age expectations in both languages, though patterns are consistent with typical bilingual development rather than a distinct articulation disorder. Given his NICU history, recurrent otitis media, and bilingual language exposure, his overall profile reflects a combination of biological risk factors and typical second-language acquisition patterns, though the magnitude of delay across both languages supports the need for continued intervention. Pragmatic skills, including initiation and turn-taking with peers, are emerging but remain below age expectations, contributing to social frustration observed both at [[DAYCARE|Little Sprouts Early Learning Center]] and at home.

3. Collaborate with Ms. [[TEACHER|Lopez]] at [[DAYCARE|Little Sprouts Early Learning Center]] to implement visual supports and peer-mediated communication strategies within the Caterpillar classroom.
