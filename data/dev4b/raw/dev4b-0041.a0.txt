**Impresiones Clínicas / Clinical Impressions**

Based on today's results, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a mixed receptive-expressive language disorder with an accompanying moderate phonological disorder, affecting both his Spanish and English, consistent with a primary language impairment rather than a difference attributable to bilingual exposure alone. His receptive skills are a relative strength compared to his expressive language, and his phonological error patterns are consistent and rule-governed, which is a good prognostic sign — this means therapy approaches that target pattern-based change, rather than sound-by-sound drilling alone, should be effective.

We recommend a combined approach using principles from [[KEEP|Hanen]] (specifically the "It Takes Two to Talk" framework for caregiver coaching) alongside a [[KEEP|Cycles]] phonological approach to target his error patterns in a structured but playful way. Given his strong imitation skills and emerging gestural communication, we will also introduce light visual supports drawn from [[KEEP|PECS]] principles to reduce frustration during transitions, though full AAC implementation is not indicated at this time. We will reassess the appropriateness of a brief trial of [[KEEP|PROMPT]] tactile cueing techniques if progress on speech sound accuracy plateaus over the next three months.
