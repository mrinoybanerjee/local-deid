IMPRESIONES / IMPRESSIONS

[[CHILD|Chuy]] presents with a bilingual language disorder affecting both Spanish, his dominant language, and English, his emerging second language, as evidenced by below-average scores across standardized measures administered in both languages and corroborated by consistent parent, teacher, and clinician observation. Because deficits are present across both of [[CHILD|Chuy]]'s languages rather than isolated to the less-dominant one, a true language disorder is indicated rather than a typical second-language acquisition pattern. Expressive language is more significantly impaired than receptive language, and mild speech sound errors are consistent with his developmental stage and exposure pattern, not indicative of a primary speech sound disorder.

[[PARENT|Guadalupe Martínez-Flores]] and [[PARENT|Pedro del Río]] demonstrate strong engagement and insight, and the extended family, including [[FAMILY|Carmen Soto]] and [[FAMILY|Teo Flores]], provides consistent additional language exposure, which is a protective factor. Coordination between [[PROVIDER|Katherine Simmons]], [[PROVIDER|Megan Daniels]], and [[PROVIDER|Brian Walsh]] should continue given overlapping fine and gross motor goals that may affect attention during structured language tasks.
