**Clinical Impressions**

Based on today's results, parent report, and classroom observations, [[CHILD|Teo]] presents with a Mixed Expressive-Receptive Language Disorder, more pronounced in expressive language, along with a Speech Sound Disorder affecting intelligibility. His difficulties are present across both English and Spanish, which rules out typical second-language acquisition as the explanation and instead points to a true underlying language and speech disorder. This pattern is consistent with ICD-10 codes [[KEEP|F80.2]] (mixed receptive-expressive language disorder) and [[KEEP|F80.0]] (speech sound disorder), billed today under CPT codes [[KEEP|92523]] and [[KEEP|92522]].
