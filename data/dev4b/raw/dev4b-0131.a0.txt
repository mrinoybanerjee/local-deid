IMPRESSIONS

Based on standardized testing, interpreter-supported interview, and classroom observation, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a mixed receptive-expressive language disorder affecting both Spanish and English, consistent with [[KEEP|ICD-10]] diagnosis [[KEEP|F80.2]]. His speech sound disorder reduces intelligibility in both languages, though relative strengths are seen in Spanish, his primary home language. This presentation is not attributable to hearing loss, oral-motor dysfunction, or lack of language exposure, given consistent bilingual input at home from [[PARENT|Lucia Vega]] and [[PARENT|Carlos Cruz]] and consistent English-language exposure at [[DAYCARE|Little Learners Daycare]].

[[CHILD|Mateo]] continues to qualify for speech-language services under both educational and medical criteria. His difficulties with intelligibility, combined with limited expressive vocabulary across both languages, place him at risk for frustration-related behaviors and reduced peer interaction, as already observed with [[PEER|Emma]] and other classmates in Ms. [[TEACHER|Lopez]]'s room.
