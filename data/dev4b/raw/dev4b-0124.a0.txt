**Impresiones / Impressions**

Based on standardized testing, parent and teacher report, and direct observation, [[CHILD|Chuy]] presents with a mixed receptive-expressive language delay (ICD-10 F80.2) alongside a moderate speech sound disorder (ICD-10 F80.0) affecting both his English and Spanish phonological systems. His difficulties appear consistent across both languages, which supports a true bilingual language disorder rather than typical second-language acquisition patterns or a language difference related to limited English exposure. His relative strength in single-word vocabulary compared to grammar and sentence-level expressive language, along with his widespread phonological process use, suggests these delays will not resolve without targeted intervention.

[[CHILD|Chuy]]'s strong nonverbal communication skills, social engagement, and stimulability for several target sounds with cueing are positive prognostic indicators. His family's consistent, warm, language-rich home environment — in Spanish, English, or a mix of both — is also a significant strength to build on, not a barrier to address, and I want to be clear with you both: raising [[CHILD|Chuy]] bilingüe is not causing his delay and should continue.
