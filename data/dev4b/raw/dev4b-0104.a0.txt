**Impressions / Impresiones**

[[CHILD|Chabela]] presents with a moderate to severe expressive language delay and a mild-to-moderate receptive language delay, affecting both Spanish and emerging English, consistent across formal measures and informal observation. Her [[KEEP|PLS-5 Spanish]] scores fall well below age expectations (Total Language Score [[KEEP|63]], [[KEEP|1st percentile]]), and her limited expressive vocabulary, inconsistent word combinations, and reliance on gesture support this impression. Articulation findings on the [[KEEP|GFTA-3]] (standard score [[KEEP|70]]) indicate a co-occurring phonological disorder, with error patterns (final consonant deletion, cluster reduction, [[KEEP|[w]]] for [[KEEP|/ɾ/]] substitution) that are somewhat atypical in frequency for age [[KEEP|2;11]], even accounting for typical bilingual developmental variation.

Given that [[CHILD|Chabela]] is being raised in an environment where Spanish is the dominant input language, with English introduced primarily through [[DAYCARE|Little Sprouts Bilingual Daycare]], her delays appear to reflect a true language disorder rather than typical second-language acquisition patterns, as deficits are present across both languages and in nonlinguistic pragmatic domains (Rossetti age equivalents of [[KEEP|20]] and [[KEEP|22 months]]). [[CHILD|Chabela]]'s stimulability for word approximations with [[KEEP|moderate]] and [[KEEP|minimal cues]] is a positive prognostic sign.

Family strengths include strong engagement from both [[PARENT|Lupita]] and [[PARENT|Pepe]], consistent routines involving extended family such as [[FAMILY|Rosa Soto]] and cousin [[PEER|Teo]], and a supportive sibling, [[SIBLING|Chuy]], who already scaffolds [[CHILD|Chabela]]'s communication attempts.
