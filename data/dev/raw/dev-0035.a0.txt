EVALUACIONES RECIENTES / RECENT EVALUATIONS

Speech-language evaluation was completed by [[PROVIDER|Ana López, M.S., CCC-SLP]] on [[DATE|September 2, 2026]] using the Rossetti Infant-Toddler Language Scale (administered bilingually with parent and grandmother report), the Goldman-Fristoe Test of Articulation-3 (screening items only, given limited expressive sample), and the Preschool Language Scale, as well as informal play-based observation con la mamá y la abuela presentes.

On the Rossetti, [[CHILD|María José]] (chronological age 2;5 at testing) performed within the 9-12 month range for expressive language and the 15-18 month range for receptive language, reflecting a significant gap between comprehension and production in both languages. Parent report indicated a combined expressive vocabulary of approximately 12 words across Spanish and English ("mamá," "papá," "aba" for agua, "no," "más," "ta-ta" for gracias, "up," "bye," "eh-eh" used as a general request, among others). She demonstrated use of consistent vowel approximations and some early consonants but no two-word combinations.

Occupational therapy evaluation by [[PROVIDER|Grace Thompson, OTR/L]], also on [[DATE|September 2, 2026]], used the Bayley Scales of Infant and Toddler Development, Fourth Edition, and the Peabody Developmental Motor Scales, Second Edition. [[CHILD|María José]] scored within the average range for gross motor skills (standard score 97) and low average for fine motor skills (standard score 83), with mild difficulty in bilateral hand use noted during block play and self-feeding with a spoon. Cognitive subtests on the Bayley were within the average range, standard score 94, 35th percentile, consistent with age expectations and supporting that her primary area of concern is expressive communication rather than global delay.

A receptive vocabulary screening using select items from the Peabody Picture Vocabulary Test-5, presented bilingually, suggested receptive skills closer to age level than expressive skills, though full standardized administration was not possible given [[CHILD|María José]]'s limited sitting tolerance and attention during the session, which lasted from [[TIME|9:15 a.m.]] to [[TIME|10:40 a.m.]].

RESULTADOS / OUTCOMES

Outcome 1 (Family priority: functional communication). [[CHILD|María José]] will use at least 10 new single words or approximations across Spanish and English, functionally and spontaneously, to request preferred items, within 6 months.
Strategy: [[PROVIDER|Ana López]] will model target words at the slow rate using simultaneous bilingual input (Spanish primary, English secondary) during play-based sessions, using cueing levels ranging from minimal verbal cue to moderate cues (mod cues) with hand-over-hand pointing as needed. The family and [[FAMILY|Rosa Mejía]] will be coached to pause expectantly after offering choices, rather than anticipating [[CHILD|María José]]'s wants, to create communication opportunities during daily routines such as snack time and bath time.

Outcome 2 (Family priority: reducing frustration). [[CHILD|María José]] will use a gesture or vocalization paired with eye contact to protest or request, in place of a full tantrum, in at least 4 of 5 observed opportunities within 4 months.
Strategy: Introduction of a small core set of manual signs (más, no, ayuda) paired with spoken words in both languages, modeled by [[PROVIDER|Ana López]] and reinforced by [[PARENT|Lucía Rodríguez Mejía]], [[PARENT|Carlos Hernández Vega]], and [[FAMILY|Rosa Mejía]] during identified high-frustration routines (mealtime, transition out of bath). [[SIBLING|Mateo]] will be included in one session to learn how to wait for his sister's attempt before responding to her cues.

Outcome 3 (Family priority: school readiness). [[CHILD|María José]] will participate in short structured listening and turn-taking activities for up to 5 minutes with an adult or peer, in preparation for group settings such as [[DAYCARE|Pequeños Pasos Bilingual Childcare]] and future Part B preschool.
Strategy: [[PROVIDER|Ana López]] will coordinate with daycare staff, with written parental consent, to share strategies used in session; [[PROVIDER|Jennifer Alvarez]] will begin transition planning discussions no later than [[DATE|January 2027]] given [[CHILD|María José]]'s third birthday in [[DATE|April 2027]].
