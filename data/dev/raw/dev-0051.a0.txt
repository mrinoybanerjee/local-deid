**Assessment Results**

At the most recent evaluation on [[DATE|November 19, 2025]], [[CHILD|María José]] was administered the Rossetti Infant-Toddler Language Scale in both English and Spanish, with receptive language scoring in the 25th percentile and expressive language scoring in the 9th percentile relative to same-age peers, consistent with a significant expressive delay and borderline receptive delay. The Bayley Scales of Infant and Toddler Development-4 yielded a Cognitive composite standard score of 95 and a Language composite standard score of 76, with the Expressive Communication subtest falling more than 2 standard deviations below the mean. The Goldman-Fristoe Test of Articulation-3 was attempted informally given her limited expressive output; stimulability for /p/, /t/, and [w] was noted, but /k/ was consistently produced as [t], and final consonants were frequently omitted, coded as [EW] on several single-word attempts. The Peabody Developmental Motor Scales-3 indicated age-appropriate fine and gross motor skills, ruling out motor-based explanations for the oral-motor patterns observed.

During today's review, informal language sampling showed continued gains: [[CHILD|María José]] produced spontaneous two-word combinations in both languages, including "más leche," glossed as [tete], and "no quiero," approximately 8 out of 10 attempts intelligible to familiar listeners, up from roughly 40% intelligibility at the prior IFSP. Her diagnosis remains consistent with Developmental Language Disorder (ICD-10 F80.1), and current services are billed under CPT 92523 for combined speech sound and language evaluation, with ongoing treatment billed under CPT 92507.
