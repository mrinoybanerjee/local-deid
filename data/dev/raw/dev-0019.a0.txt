Based on these findings, the team, including [[PARENT|Lucía Rodríguez]], [[PARENT|Héctor Flores]], [[PROVIDER|Dana Whitfield]], and [[PROVIDER|Priya Desai]], developed and reviewed three outcomes for the coming IFSP period. The second outcome addresses reduction of frustration behaviors, specifically pulling, crying, and hitting, that occur when [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s communication attempts are not understood. The family will track frustration episodes in a simple daily log to be reviewed at the next contact, and [[PROVIDER|Priya Desai]] will continue direct intervention sessions twice monthly to model the pause-and-expand strategy in vivo.
