IMPRESSIONS / IMPRESIONES DIAGNÓSTICAS
- Results of this evaluation indicate that [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a moderate-to-severe speech sound disorder (phonological) affecting both Spanish and English, characterized by consistent, cross-linguistic error patterns including stopping of fricatives, cluster reduction, liquid simplification, and final consonant deletion.
- Concurrently, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a language disorder affecting both receptive and expressive language in Spanish and English, consistent with a bilingual child exhibiting difficulties beyond what would be expected from typical second-language acquisition or dialectal variation alone, as error patterns are present and proportionally similar across both languages.
- Diagnostic impression: Speech Sound Disorder (ICD-10: F80.0) and Language Disorder (ICD-10: F80.2), affecting a bilingual Spanish-English speaker, with patterns not attributable to dialectal difference or typical bilingual language acquisition.
- Severity is considered moderate-to-severe based on standard scores falling at or below the 4th percentile across multiple measures and estimated connected-speech intelligibility of 40% to unfamiliar listeners.
- These findings are consistent with, and represent an increase in severity compared to, concerns noted at the time of initial CDSA enrollment in [[DATE|April 2023]].
- [[CHILD|Mateo]] continues to meet eligibility criteria for ongoing early intervention services under [[AGENCY|Wake County CDSA]] guidelines based on documented delay exceeding 25% in two or more developmental domains (communication and, to a lesser extent, social-emotional functioning related to communication frustration).
