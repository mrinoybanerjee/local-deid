CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS / IMPRESIONES CLÍNICAS
- Results indicate a significant delay in both receptive and expressive language skills across [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s combined linguistic system (Spanish and English), consistent with a diagnosis of Mixed Receptive-Expressive Language Disorder (ICD-10: F80.2).
- Concurrent speech sound production delays are present in both languages, characterized by phonological process patterns (final consonant deletion, cluster reduction, velar fronting, liquid simplification) that are atypical for his age in either language, ruling out typical second-language acquisition patterns as the sole explanation.
- Intelligibility deficits, particularly with unfamiliar listeners, are functionally significant and are impacting [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s ability to communicate needs at home and at [[DAYCARE|Sunny Days Early Learning Center]].
- No evidence was found to suggest hearing loss, oral-motor dysfunction, or social communication disorder as primary contributing factors.
- [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s delays are evident across both languages, which supports a true language disorder rather than a language difference attributable to bilingual exposure alone.
- Family engagement is strong, and the home language environment, while predominantly Spanish, provides rich communicative opportunities that should be leveraged in intervention planning.
