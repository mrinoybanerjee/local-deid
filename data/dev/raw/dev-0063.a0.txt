Resultados y estrategias / Outcomes and Strategies:

Outcome 1 (Communication/Expressive Language): [[CHILD|María José]] will use two- to three-word phrases in Spanish and/or English to request desired items and express needs during daily routines (mealtime, play, dressing) across home and daycare settings, with 80% accuracy as measured by parent report and clinician observation. Strategies include: modeling expansion of single words into short phrases ("quiero jugo" instead of "jugo"); use of a bilingual visual communication board at home and at daycare developed collaboratively with Ms. [[TEACHER|Holt]]; parent coaching in both languages during the author's weekly home visits; and encouragement of both parents to continue using their strongest language (Spanish) rather than restricting input. Progress to date: [[CHILD|María José]] now spontaneously combines two words in approximately 70% of opportunities, up from 30% at the initial IFSP.

Outcome 2 (Speech Sound Production): [[CHILD|María José]] will produce word-final consonants and the sounds /k/ and /g/ in single words with 80% accuracy given minimal cues, generalizing from structured drill to spontaneous speech. Strategies include: articulation drill using minimal pairs and visual/tactile cueing (e.g., touch cues for final consonant deletion); home practice sheets translated into Spanish for parent carryover; and coordination with the family to practice target sounds during bath time and mealtime, routines the family identified as natural practice opportunities. Progress to date: as noted above, [[CHILD|María José]] achieved 8/10 (80%) accuracy for word-final consonants with moderate cues this session, meeting the first benchmark toward this outcome; she is not yet consistent without cueing support.

Outcome 3 (Family Support/Access to Services): The family will continue to access bilingual early intervention services and community resources to support [[CHILD|María José]]'s communication development and prepare for the transition to preschool services. Strategies include: continued coordination between this writer, Service Coordinator [[PROVIDER|Ana Gutiérrez]], and [[AGENCY|Fayetteville Early Head Start]] staff; a referral to [[AGENCY|Smart Start of Cumberland County]]'s bilingual parenting workshop series beginning [[DATE|el 10 de julio de 2025]]; and preparation of transition paperwork for the local school system's Child Find process, anticipated around [[CHILD|María José]]'s third birthday in [[DATE|March 2026]].
