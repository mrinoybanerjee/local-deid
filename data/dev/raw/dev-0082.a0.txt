**Resultados y Estrategias / Outcomes and Strategies**

**Outcome 1:** Teo will use 2-3 word combinations in Spanish and/or English to request desired items and actions across home and daycare settings.
*Strategy:* Model short phrases during daily routines ("más agua," "abre la puerta"); use the hand-under-hand cueing level (mod cues) taught during sessions; pause expectantly after modeling to give Teo time to imitate.
*Progress:* At the last probe, Teo produced target words such as "más" and "abre" with 7/10 accuracy during structured play, up from 3/10 at intake. His expressive vocabulary checklist score placed him at the 9th percentile for his corrected age of 2;6, reflecting continued delay but steady growth.

**Outcome 2:** Teo will follow one-step directions paired with gestures in familiar routines, in both English and Spanish, to reduce frustration during transitions.
*Strategy:* Pair verbal direction with a visual or gesture cue (for example, pointing while saying "ven aquí" or "come here"); provide praise immediately when he responds, even partially.
*Progress:* Parent report and clinic observation suggest Teo now follows familiar one-step directions with gesture support about 80% of the time, compared to roughly 40% at the initial evaluation on [[DATE|April 28, 2025]].

**Outcome 3:** Teo will increase his phonemic inventory, specifically producing initial /p/, /m/, and /t/ sounds consistently in single words, to improve intelligibility for familiar listeners.
*Strategy:* Use shaping and modeling for target sounds during snack and play routines; reinforce approximations such as "[pa]" for "papá" and "[ta]" for "Teo."
*Progress:* Teo now produces /p/ and /m/ in the initial position with 8/10 accuracy in imitation; /t/ remains emerging. His most recent quoted production, "[[CHILD|Teo]] no, mío," during a toy-sharing moment with [[SIBLING|Lupita]], shows increasing use of possessive language alongside continued sound work.
