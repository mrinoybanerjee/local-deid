IMPRESSIONS

Based on standardized scores, CG/teacher report, and direct observation, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents w/ a Language Disorder affecting both receptive and expressive language, in both Spanish and English, consistent w/ a true bilingual language impairment rather than typical second-language acquisition variability — scores fall significantly below expected limits in BOTH languages, which supports a primary language disorder rather than a language difference pattern. Co-occurring mild-moderate phonological delay noted, likely compounded by hx of recurrent OM and associated fluctuating hearing during a critical early language-learning period, though current hearing is WNL. Frustration-related behaviors appear functionally related to communication breakdown rather than a primary behavioral/sensory concern, though this should continue to be monitored.
