Current IFSP Outcomes and Strategies

Outcome [[KEEP|1]]: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will use [[KEEP|20]] functional single words across both languages during daily routines, as reported by caregivers, by the next review. Strategy: phonological therapy using the [[KEEP|Cycles]] approach, targeting final consonant deletion and stopping patterns across a rotating word list, with home practice coached in both English and Spanish. Progress: at initial baseline [[KEEP|3 of 20]] target words were produced; at this review, [[KEEP|9 of 20]] ([[KEEP|45%]]) target words are produced with [[KEEP|mod cues]] to [[KEEP|min cues]].

Outcome [[KEEP|2]]: [[PARENT|Carla]] and [[PARENT|Andrés]] will use responsive communication strategies during daily play to increase [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s initiations. Strategy: coaching sessions based on the [[KEEP|Hanen]] program [[KEEP|It Takes Two to Talk]], focused on following [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s lead, expanding his utterances ("sol" → "quiero sol" / "I want sun"), and building in wait time during routines such as snack and bath. [[PARENT|Carla]] reported, "ya sé esperar más antes de hablar por él" (I already know to wait more before speaking for him), reflecting increased carryover of strategies between sessions.

Outcome [[KEEP|3]]: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will reduce frustration-related behavior during communication breakdowns from [[KEEP|8]] episodes per day to [[KEEP|3]] or fewer, as tracked by caregiver log. Strategy: introduction of a core board and [[KEEP|PECS]] exchange cards as a backup modality for high-demand moments (snack, more, help, outside), paired with [[KEEP|PROMPT]] tactile-kinesthetic cueing to support jaw and lip placement for imitated words during structured sessions. Progress: caregiver log shows a decrease from [[KEEP|8]] to [[KEEP|4]] daily episodes over the past [[KEEP|6 weeks]], with [[CHILD|Mateo]] independently retrieving the "more" card [[KEEP|7 of 10]] opportunities.

Outcome [[KEEP|4]]: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will engage in reciprocal play routines with [[SIBLING|Diego]] and [[SIBLING|Sol]] for at least [[KEEP|5 minutes]] without adult prompting, [[KEEP|3]] times per week. Strategy: structured sibling play sessions incorporating [[KEEP|PECS]] cards and simple turn-taking scripts, with elements of the [[KEEP|Kaufman]] (Kaufman Speech to Language Protocol) hierarchy used to shape approximations of play-related words such as "mi turno" (my turn) and his sister's name. [[PARENT|Andrés]] noted, "[[SIBLING|Sol]] ya sabe esperar y le enseña las tarjetas" (Sol already knows to wait and shows him the cards), indicating generalization of strategies to sibling interaction outside of therapy.
