IMPRESSIONS

- Based on standardized testing, parent and teacher report, and direct observation, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a mixed receptive-expressive language disorder ([[KEEP|F80.2]]) and a moderate-to-severe phonological disorder ([[KEEP|F80.0]]).
- Deficits are present across both languages, which rules out typical second-language acquisition as the primary explanation for his profile; this supports a true language disorder rather than a language difference.
- [[CHILD|Teo]]'s reduced intelligibility and limited expressive language are significantly impacting his participation with peers, including [[PEER|Ethan]], and his ability to be understood by unfamiliar listeners such as extended family and community members.
- Prognosis for improvement with consistent, bilingual intervention is considered good, given [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s strong engagement, intact hearing, and supportive family involvement.
- Continued monitoring of middle ear status is recommended given the history of recurrent otitis media and tube placement.

RECOMMENDATIONS

- [[CHILD|Mateo]] qualifies for and should receive individual speech-language therapy services twice weekly for [[KEEP|30]] minutes per session, targeting phonological processes and expressive syntax, provided by a bilingual clinician through [[EMPLOYER|TBSLT]].
- Treatment should use a combination of [[KEEP|Cycles]] phonological approach for speech sound production and [[KEEP|Hanen]]-based strategies to support expressive language in daily routines.
- A trial of [[KEEP|PROMPT]] techniques is recommended to address persistent substitution patterns affecting intelligibility.
- Continued coordination with [[DAYCARE|Little Sprouts Daycare and Preschool]] staff, including Ms. [[TEACHER|Lopez]] and Mr. [[TEACHER|Nguyen]], is recommended to support carryover of targets into the classroom.
