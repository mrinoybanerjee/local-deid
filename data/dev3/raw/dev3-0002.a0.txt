9. DIAGNOSTIC IMPRESSIONS

Based on results across both languages, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a bilingual speech sound disorder (phonological) and expressive-receptive language delay affecting both Spanish and English, consistent with ICD-10 code [[KEEP|F80.9]] (developmental disorder of speech and language, unspecified) and [[KEEP|F80.1]] (expressive language disorder). Because deficits are present and proportionate across both of [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s languages, these findings are not attributable to typical second-language acquisition or bilingual language development alone. [[CHILD|Mateo]] continues to meet eligibility criteria for Part C early intervention services under North Carolina's Infant-Toddler Program, pending his transition planning given his age.
