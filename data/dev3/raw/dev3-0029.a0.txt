Impressions

Based on standardized testing, parent and teacher report, and clinical observation, [[CHILD|Teo]] presents with a moderate expressive and mild-to-moderate receptive language delay affecting both English and Spanish, along with a moderate speech sound disorder characterized by fronting, gliding, and cluster reduction patterns. Because his scores were low across both languages and his error patterns are consistent in Spanish and English, these difficulties appear to reflect a true language and speech disorder rather than typical second-language learning patterns. His strong social and pragmatic skills, warmth with peers like [[PEER|Diego]], and good nonverbal problem-solving are real strengths that will support his progress in therapy.

[[CHILD|Teo]] meets eligibility criteria for ongoing early intervention services under his current IFSP, and I recommend that his team consider adding or adjusting specific speech-language goals, detailed below.
