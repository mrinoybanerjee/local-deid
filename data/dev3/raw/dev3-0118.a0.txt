## 7. IFSP OUTCOMES AND STRATEGIES

### Outcome [[KEEP|#1]]: Expressive Communication
*[[CHILD|Mateo]] will use single words and early word combinations to request preferred items and actions across home and child care settings.*

**Criteria:** [[CHILD|Mateo]] will independently produce at least [[KEEP|75 different words]] and begin combining two words (e.g., "más agua") in [[KEEP|4 of 5]] observed opportunities, as measured by caregiver log and direct observation, by the next review.

**Strategies:**
- Model target words at a one-word-above level during daily routines (expansion).
- Use expectant waiting of [[KEEP|5 seconds]] before offering a model.
- Offer structured choices during mealtime and play to elicit verbal selection rather than reaching.
- Coach [[FAMILY|Abuela Rosa]] and both parents on expansion and waiting strategies during home visits.

**Progress to date:** [[CHILD|Mateo]] has increased from an estimated [[KEEP|30 words]] at intake to [[KEEP|45 words]] at this review (see Section 4.1). Progress is rated as "making progress, continue current strategies."

### Outcome [[KEEP|#2]]: Reducing Communicative Frustration
*[[CHILD|Mateo]] will use an alternative communication attempt (word, approximation, or gesture) instead of a physical protest behavior when he wants something.*

**Criteria:** Reduction of protest behaviors to [[KEEP|1 of 6]] or fewer observed opportunities, with a corresponding increase in communicative attempts, by the next review.

**Strategies:**
- Teach and model a simple sign ("more") paired with the spoken word in both languages.
- Caregivers to narrate [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s intent before he becomes frustrated ("quieres el carro, dime 'carro'").
- Consistency across [[DAYCARE|Little Sprouts Early Head Start Center]] and home environments; [[TEACHER|Brittany Sawyer]] will use the same sign and verbal model in the classroom.

**Progress to date:** Protest behaviors reduced from [[KEEP|4 of 6]] to [[KEEP|2 of 6]] observed opportunities. Progress is rated as "making progress."

### Outcome [[KEEP|#3]]: Peer Engagement
*[[CHILD|Mateo]] will initiate brief interactions with peers during play activities at child care.*

**Criteria:** [[CHILD|Mateo]] will initiate a peer interaction (offering a toy, joint attention bid) in [[KEEP|3 of 5]] observed play opportunities.

**Strategies:**
- Adult-facilitated joint play with one peer at a time during a preferred activity.
- Narrate peer actions and emotions to build social understanding.
- Use visual supports for turn-taking (a simple "my turn / your turn" picture card).

**Progress to date:** This outcome was added at this review based on [[TEACHER|Brittany Sawyer]]'s updated report; baseline data collection is in progress.
