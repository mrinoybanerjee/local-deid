**Impressions**

Taken together, [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s results indicate a mixed receptive-expressive language delay, with expressive language more significantly affected than receptive language, in the context of typical bilingual development. His low scores on English-only and Spanish-only measures, combined with his stronger performance when both languages are considered together, support ruling out typical bilingual variation as the sole explanation for his delay. His speech sound patterns are also beyond what would be expected purely from bilingual phonological development at his age.

Diagnostic impression: Mixed expressive-receptive language disorder ([[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.2]]) with an associated phonological disorder ([[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.0]]), affecting both English and Spanish. [[CHILD|Mateo]] continues to qualify for early intervention services through the [[AGENCY|CDSA]] system under North Carolina's eligibility criteria for communication delay.

Given [[PARENT|Lucía]]'s report of loud snoring and mouth breathing, we also recommend a referral to an ear, nose, and throat specialist to rule out enlarged tonsils or adenoids as a contributing factor, and we have shared this concern with [[PROVIDER|Dr. Patel]]'s office directly.
