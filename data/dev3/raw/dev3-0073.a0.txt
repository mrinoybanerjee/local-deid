IMPRESSIONS / IMPRESIONES

- [[CHILD|María José Hernández Pérez]] presents with a speech sound disorder and a language disorder affecting both Spanish and English, consistent with a diagnosis of Developmental Language Disorder rather than typical bilingual language difference, given deficits present across both languages.
- Her error patterns on the [[KEEP|GFTA-3]] (fronting, final consonant deletion) are not explained by typical Spanish-English phonological transfer and reflect a true speech sound disorder.
- Receptive and expressive scores fell below the expected range in both languages, supporting a true language disorder rather than a language difference secondary to bilingual exposure alone.
- Code-switching observed during the sample is a typical and expected bilingual behavior and should not be targeted for reduction.
- [[CHILD|Cocó]]'s limited verbal initiation at [[DAYCARE|Honeycomb Hollow Preschool]] appears secondary to her expressive language and intelligibility deficits rather than a primary social or pragmatic disorder.
- Family and teacher reports are consistent with standardized testing and classroom observation.
