CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS:
Based on standardized testing, parent/CG report, and clinical observation, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents w/ a Language Disorder affecting both Spanish (primary/dominant lang) and English, characterized by deficits in both receptive and, more significantly, expressive lang skills relative to CA expectations. Scores fall consistently in the disordered range across both domains and both langs, supporting a primary lang impairment rather than a lang-difference-only profile often seen in typically-developing bilingual children (i.e., deficits present across BOTH langs, not just the non-dominant one).

Pt also presents w/ a concomitant Speech Sound Disorder; phonological patterns (final consonant deletion, liquid gliding, stopping) are present across both langs, consistent w/ a phonological, rather than purely articulation-based, etiology. Pt's hx of recurrent OM w/ PE tube placement on [[DATE|March 2, 2024]] may have been a contributing factor historically, though current hearing is WNL per recent screening. Mild sensory-seeking behaviors noted during session (per above) may warrant OT screening; this was discussed w/ [[PARENT|Lucía Flores]] and will be relayed to [[PROVIDER|Denise Walker]] at [[AGENCY|Piedmont CDSA]] for consideration at the next IFSP review.

Pt's intelligibility deficits, combined vocab delay, and MLU below age expectation support a dx of Mixed Receptive-Expressive Language Disorder (ICD-10 [[KEEP|F80.2]]) w/ comorbid Phonological Disorder (ICD-10 [[KEEP|F80.0]]). Prognosis is judged good w/ consistent intervention, given pt's strong social engagement, imitation skills, functional play, and supportive family involvement.
