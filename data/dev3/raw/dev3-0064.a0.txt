IMPRESIONES CLÍNICAS / CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS

Based on standardized testing, parent and teacher report, and clinical observation, [[CHILD|María José Hernández Flores]] presents with a mixed expressive-receptive language disorder affecting both Spanish and English, along with a moderate speech sound disorder characterized by phonological process errors and inconsistent articulation across both languages. Her difficulties are not attributable solely to bilingual language exposure; errors were noted across both linguistic systems and exceed what would be expected for a typically developing bilingual child of her age and language exposure history.

[[CHILD|Majo]]'s receptive language skills, while below average, are notably stronger than her expressive skills in both languages, a pattern consistent with a primarily expressive language disorder with secondary receptive involvement. Her speech intelligibility, estimated at approximately [[KEEP|40%]] to unfamiliar listeners and [[KEEP|70%]] to familiar listeners, significantly impacts her ability to communicate basic needs and participate in classroom and home routines.

ICD-10 code: [[KEEP|F80.4]] (Mixed receptive-expressive language disorder) and [[KEEP|F80.0]] (Phonological disorder). CPT code for evaluation: [[KEEP|92523]].
