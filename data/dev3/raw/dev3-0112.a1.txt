summary and clinical impressions

based on parent report direct testing and language sample analysis majo presents with a moderate expressive and receptive language delay affecting both spanish and english along with a moderate phonological disorder that further reduces her overall intelligibility majo's language skills are weaker than expected in both of her languages which supports a true language disorder rather than a language difference related to typical bilingual development or typical second language acquisition this distinction matters because majo's deficits are present and roughly comparable across both languages rather than isolated to the less dominant one

the pattern of sound errors including final consonant deletion cluster reduction and liquid substitutions in both languages is consistent with a phonological delay that would be expected to respond to structured intervention majo's stimulability on several target sounds with [[KEEP|moderate cues]] is a positive prognostic sign

majo's social communication and play skills are a relative strength and support a good foundation for intervention mom and dad are highly engaged and motivated which is also a strong protective factor the family's current connection to the [[AGENCY|CDSA]] and to service coordinator [[PROVIDER|Carmen Ríos]] provides a good existing structure to build continued services around
