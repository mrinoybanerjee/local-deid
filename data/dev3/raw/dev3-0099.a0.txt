Outcomes and Strategies / Resultados y Estrategias

Outcome [[KEEP|1]]: [[CHILD|Teo]] will use [[KEEP|2-3]] word combinations to request preferred items and activities in English or Spanish across home and daycare settings.
Strategies: [[PROVIDER|Ana Torres]] will model expansion using [[KEEP|Hanen]] "It Takes Two to Talk" strategies during biweekly home visits. [[PARENT|Lucía]] and [[PARENT|Héctor]] will practice following [[CHILD|Teo]]'s lead and pausing expectantly before offering preferred items, both in Spanish and English, including during play with [[PET|Rocky]] and with [[SIBLING|Sol]].

Outcome [[KEEP|2]]: [[CHILD|Teo]] will increase intelligibility of early-developing consonants (/[[KEEP|m]]/, /[[KEEP|b]]/, /[[KEEP|p]]/, /[[KEEP|s]]/) in single words across [[KEEP|3 of 5]] opportunities with [[KEEP|minimal cues]].
Strategies: [[PROVIDER|Ana Torres]] will use a modified [[KEEP|Cycles]] approach targeting final consonants in both languages. Home practice will embed target sounds in daily routines; [[PARENT|Lucía]] noted, "Podemos practicar con las palabras de la rutina, como 'más' y 'pan.'"

Outcome [[KEEP|3]]: [[CHILD|Teo]] will demonstrate improved fine motor coordination for self-feeding and simple manipulatives during meals and play.
Strategies: Occupational therapy consultation will continue monthly; [[PARENT|Héctor]] will incorporate utensil practice during dinner routines.

Outcome [[KEEP|4]]: The family will access respite or childcare support resources to reduce caregiver stress, per [[PARENT|Lucía]]'s report, "A veces necesitamos un descanso, especialmente cuando [[SIBLING|Sol]] también necesita atención."
Strategies: [[PROVIDER|Maria Elena Gutiérrez]] will provide a referral list for respite care through [[AGENCY|CDSA Piedmont Central]] and connect the family with the [[AGENCY|Smart Start]] parenting group coordinator.
