IMPRESSIONS

Based on today's assessment, parent and teacher report, and medical history, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a Developmental Language Disorder, with expressive language more significantly affected than receptive language, in both of his languages. This is not simply a function of learning two languages; his delays are present and comparable in both English and Spanish, which tells us this is a true language-learning difficulty rather than typical bilingual development. His speech sound production is age-appropriate once accounting for normal bilingual phonological patterns, and his social communication skills (joint attention, reciprocity, play) are a relative strength and do not raise concern for autism spectrum disorder at this time.

[[CHILD|Mateo]]'s history of recurrent ear infections may have contributed to some early language-learning difficulty, but his current hearing is normal, so ongoing speech-language intervention, not medical treatment, is the right next step.
