IMPRESSIONS

Based on standardized testing, parent and teacher report, and clinical observation, [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a mixed receptive-expressive language disorder (ICD-10 [[KEEP|F80.2]]) affecting both Spanish and English, along with a moderate speech sound disorder (ICD-10 [[KEEP|F80.0]]) characterized by final consonant deletion, cluster reduction, velar fronting, and liquid simplification. These findings are consistent across both of [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s languages, which supports a true bilingual language impairment rather than a language difference secondary to typical second-language acquisition. The recurrent otitis media history and bilateral tube placement reported by [[PARENT|Lucía Rodríguez Reyes]] and documented by [[PROVIDER|Dr. Angela Ruiz]] may have contributed to early delays, though normal hearing on the most recent audiogram suggests hearing is not currently a limiting factor. [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s strengths include social engagement, imitation skills, and stimulability for several error sounds with cueing, all of which are favorable prognostic indicators for therapy.

Given the upcoming transition to [[SCHOOL|Brookhaven Elementary School]], early and intensive intervention is strongly recommended to improve [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s intelligibility and language skills before he enters a classroom environment with less bilingual support than [[DAYCARE|Little Sprouts Early Learning Center]] currently provides.
