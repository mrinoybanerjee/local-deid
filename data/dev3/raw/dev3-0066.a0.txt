Outcomes and Strategies

[[KEEP|Outcome 1]]: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will use two-word combinations to request preferred items, actions, or attention from familiar communication partners across home and community settings, increasing from his current baseline of zero consistent two-word combinations to at least [[KEEP|8/10]] opportunities across three consecutive sessions. Strategies will include caregiver coaching modeled on [[KEEP|Hanen]]'s [[KEEP|It Takes Two to Talk]] program, emphasizing following [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s lead, expanding his single-word utterances, and building in deliberate communication temptations during snack and play routines. [[PROVIDER|Dana Whitfield]] will also use [[KEEP|PROMPT]] tactile-kinesthetic cueing during direct sessions to support jaw and lip placement for emerging two-word phrases.

[[KEEP|Outcome 2]]: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will initiate requests using a picture exchange system across at least two settings, home and the CDSA clinic, with [[KEEP|mod cues]] fading toward independence over the review period. The team will introduce [[KEEP|PECS]] (Picture Exchange Communication System) beginning with a single preferred-item exchange and progressing through discrimination training as tolerated, with a target accuracy of [[KEEP|80%]] of trials at [[KEEP|min cues]] by the next review. Both parents and [[FAMILY|Rosa]] will receive hands-on training in exchange prompting during home visits so the system can be used consistently across caregivers.

[[KEEP|Outcome 3]]: [[CHILD|Mateo]] will produce final consonants in a core set of [[KEEP|10]] functional words with [[KEEP|80%]] accuracy across two consecutive sessions, reducing the phonological pattern of final consonant deletion that currently limits intelligibility. Treatment will use the [[KEEP|Cycles]] approach, targeting final consonants in cycles of two to three weeks per sound class, supplemented by [[KEEP|Kaufman]] Speech to Language Protocol card sequences to shape simplified word shapes into more complete productions, for example moving from [[KEEP|"ba"]] toward the target [[KEEP|"bola"]].

The team also discussed, but did not yet adopt as a formal outcome, continued monitoring of [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s social communication and joint attention skills, given his history and the ICD-10 [[KEEP|F80.2]] diagnosis; [[PROVIDER|Dana Whitfield]] recommended revisiting this at the next review if gains in requesting and phonology do not generalize to spontaneous social initiations.
