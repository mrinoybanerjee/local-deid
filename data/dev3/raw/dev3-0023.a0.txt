7. CLINICAL IMPRESSIONS

Results of this evaluation indicate that [[CHILD|Mateo]] presents with a Mixed Receptive-Expressive Language Disorder and a Speech Sound Disorder affecting intelligibility in both English and Spanish. His standardized scores fall more than [[KEEP|1.5]] standard deviations below the mean across receptive and expressive domains, and his articulation skills are significantly delayed relative to age expectations. Diagnostic impressions are consistent with [[KEEP|ICD-10]] code [[KEEP|F80.2]] (Mixed Receptive-Expressive Language Disorder) and [[KEEP|F80.0]] (Phonological Disorder). Billing for this evaluation is reflected under CPT code [[KEEP|92523]].

[[CHILD|Mateo]]'s bilingual exposure does not account for the magnitude of his delays, as difficulties are present across both languages per parent report and classroom observation from la maestra [[TEACHER|Carmen]]. Family and caregiver involvement, including from doña [[FAMILY|Lupe]] and [[PARENT|Carlos]], remains a clear strength supporting ongoing intervention, as does [[CHILD|Mateo]]'s motivation during structured play tasks.
