### 11.2 Impressions

Findings support a mixed receptive-expressive language disorder, [[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.2]], and a speech sound disorder, [[KEEP|ICD-10 F80.0]]. The language conclusion rests on functional difficulties across languages, limited message organization, and agreement between caregiver and classroom observations.

Strengths include social interest, shared attention, imaginative play, gesture use, caregiver involvement, and response to teaching. Prognosis for functional improvement is favorable with consistent intervention and coordinated practice. The rate of progress will require direct measurement over time.

### 13.1 Caregiver strategies

Practice short contrasts such as [[KEEP|"key"]], [[KEEP|"tea"]], [[KEEP|"go"]], and [[KEEP|"dough"]], only when the treating clinician selects them. Keep practice brief and successful. Do not require repeated correction during every conversation.

### 13.2 Classroom strategies
